Understanding Sexual Health and the Role of Urgent Care

Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) are remarkably common. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), millions of new infections occur nationwide every year, spanning all age groups, backgrounds, and relationship statuses. Many common STIs—such as chlamydia and gonorrhea—frequently develop with no noticeable symptoms in their early stages.

Important

Because symptoms are not always present, routine screening is vital for anyone who is sexually active.

Traditionally, individuals seeking testing had to schedule appointments weeks in advance with primary care providers or navigate complex public health clinic hours. Today, urgent care STD testing bridges that gap, offering walk-in convenience, extended operating hours (including evenings and Saturdays), and rapid access to professional medical evaluation.

1. Chlamydia and Gonorrhea

Overview
These bacterial infections are among the most frequently reported STIs in the United States.
Symptoms
Often asymptomatic (“silent infections”), but may cause unusual discharge, painful urination, or pelvic discomfort.
Testing Method
Typically diagnosed quickly and painlessly via a simple urine sample or swab. Both are easily treated and cured with antibiotics when caught early.

2. HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus)

Overview
HIV attacks the body’s immune system, specifically CD4 cells. Early detection is critical for starting antiretroviral therapy (ART), which allows individuals to live long, healthy lives and achieve viral suppression.
Testing Method
Blood testing (either rapid point-of-care testing or standard lab draws). Depending on the type of test, it is important to discuss your “window period”—the time between potential exposure and when a test can accurately detect antibodies or antigens—with your clinician.

3. Syphilis

Overview
A bacterial infection that progresses in stages if left untreated. The primary stage often features a single, painless sore (chancre) where the bacteria entered the body.
Testing Method
Blood test. Early identification is vital because untreated syphilis can lead to severe cardiovascular and neurological complications over time.

4. Herpes (HSV-1 and HSV-2)

Overview
Viral infections that can cause recurring outbreaks of painful blisters or sores, though many infected individuals experience very mild or unrecognized symptoms.
Testing Method
Evaluation can involve blood antibody testing or a direct lesion swab if active blisters or sores are present.

5. Hepatitis B and Hepatitis C

Overview
Viral infections that affect liver function. Hepatitis B and C can be transmitted through sexual contact or blood exposure.
Testing Method
Specific blood antibody and antigen panels.

What to Expect During Your Visit at Sahara West Urgent Care

We understand that visiting an urgent care clinic for sensitive health concerns can cause anxiety. Our team is dedicated to providing compassionate, respectful, and transparent care from the moment you walk through our doors.

  1. Step 1: Walk In or Check In Online

    You do not need an advance appointment. Visit our facility at 6125 West Sahara Avenue, Las Vegas, NV 89146, during regular operating hours (Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM–3:00 PM). You can also choose to book ahead online if you prefer a scheduled time.

  2. Step 2: Confidential Clinical Consultation

    You will meet privately with an experienced healthcare provider. They will ask relevant, non-judgmental questions regarding:

    • Recent sexual activity or potential exposures
    • Any physical symptoms you may be noticing (such as rashes, sores, pain, or discharge)
    • The timing of any potential exposure (to ensure tests are ordered at the clinically appropriate time)

  3. Step 3: Simple Sample Collection

    Depending on the specific panels recommended by your provider, testing is straightforward and involves minimal discomfort. Samples may include:

    • A simple urine collection
    • A routine blood draw
    • A gentle swab (genital, throat, or rectal, depending on clinical evaluation and risk factors)

  4. Step 4: Results and Follow-Up Care

    Results timing

    While walk-in visits and sample collections happen same-day, laboratory turnaround times vary depending on the specific panel ordered. Most standard STI results are available within 24 to 48 hours.

    Our team contacts you as soon as your results are finalized to discuss what they mean, whether follow-up testing is needed, and whether treatment or prescriptions should be initiated.

FAQs

1. Do I need an appointment for same-day STD testing in Las Vegas?

No appointment is necessary. Sahara West Urgent Care welcomes walk-in patients during normal business hours for fast evaluation and testing. You may also book ahead online if you prefer a structured time slot.

2. How long do STI test results take?

While your evaluation and sample collection happen the same day you walk in, laboratory turnaround times depend on the specific tests performed. Most standard results are available within 24 to 48 hours.

3. Is my visit completely confidential?

Yes. All patient visits, medical consultations, and test results are fully protected by strict HIPAA privacy regulations and handled with complete discretion.

4. Can I get treated at the clinic if my test is positive?

Yes. One of the primary benefits of choosing Sahara West Urgent Care is our ability to provide medical care under one roof. If your test returns positive, our providers can discuss treatment options, prescribe medications, and offer guidance on partner notification during a follow-up consultation.

5. What should I bring with me to my visit?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), a form of payment for co-pays or self-pay services, and a list of any current medications or relevant medical history.

6. Do I need to choose which tests I want before arriving?

Not at all. You do not need to self-diagnose. Our clinical providers will talk through your history and symptoms to recommend the most accurate testing panel for your situation.
